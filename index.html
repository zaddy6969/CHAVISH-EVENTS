A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WONDER

Before we begin.

Can I plan an event if I don’t have a venue yet?

Yes. Start with your occasion, city, guest count and approximate budget. These details help narrow down a suitable venue and the kind of experience you want.

How early should I start planning?

For a large wedding or a busy seasonal date, begin several months ahead. Smaller gatherings can need less time. Your event size, venue and production requirements will shape the schedule.

Can the event be designed around my budget?

Your budget is the starting point for the plan. Prioritise the details that matter most to you, then decide on the venue, décor, entertainment and guest experience together.

What happens when I create an event brief?

Your answers become a brief you can download or copy. It is not a booking or an enquiry submission. Download or copy your brief and contact CHAVISH EVENTS by WhatsApp, phone, or email.
